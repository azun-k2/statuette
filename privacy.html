Statuette Privacy Policy

Statuette collects nothing and makes no network connections.

The short version

Statuette collects no data at all. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network access of any kind. Nothing leaves your Mac, because nothing is ever sent.

What the app reads

Statuette reads statistics that macOS publishes about your own computer: processor load, memory use, free space on mounted disks, bytes sent and received by network interfaces, battery charge and condition, and, with the optional helper, temperature and fan sensors.

These readings are displayed and then discarded. The app keeps a two-minute history in memory to draw its small graphs. That history is never written to disk and is gone when you quit.

Statuette only reads. It never writes to your Mac's system management controller, and it cannot change fan speeds or any other setting of your hardware.

What is stored on your Mac

Your preferences, which items appear in the menu bar, your alert thresholds, colour theme and text size, are saved by macOS in the app's own preferences, on your Mac. They are not transmitted anywhere.

Statuette Sensors

The companion helper follows the same rules. It reads temperature and fan sensors, writes the readings to a shared container on your Mac so Statuette can display them, and makes no network connection either.
